## National trend (all reporting utilities, customer-weighted)
| Year | SAIDI with MED | SAIDI without MED | SAIFI with MED |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 215.7 | 105.5 | 1.20 |
| 2017 | 473.1 | 114.5 | 1.39 |
| 2020 | 491.9 | 119.2 | 1.44 |
| 2024 | **611.3** (≈10.2 h, highest in the series) | 126.0 | 1.52 |
| 2025 | 262.6 | 120.9 | 1.34 |

- Routine (without-MED) outage minutes rose from 105.5 (2013) to 120.9 (2025), +14.6%. 33 of 51 jurisdictions had a higher 2023–25 average than 2013–15 average.
- Total minutes swing with storm years: 2024's 611 minutes was 2.8× 2013. 2024's top states were South Carolina (3,137), Maine (1,749), North Carolina (1,441), Florida (1,321) and Texas (1,271).
- Across all state-years, major event days account for a median 46.7% of outage minutes.
- 2025 leaders: West Virginia 1,013, Michigan 672, Nebraska 547, Alaska 541, Mississippi 501.
